**Answer**
To cancel a waitlisted segment in a PNR, use the **WPNC** format.

**Format & explanation**
`WPNC‡LAX/ORD‡12JAN‡HK1`
- `LAX/ORD` – city pair (origin/destination) of the segment
- `12JAN` – date of the segment
- `HK1` – number of seats to cancel (e.g., 1 seat)

**Example**
`WPNC‡LAX/ORD‡12JAN‡HK1` – removes one waitlisted seat on the Los Angeles‑to‑Chicago segment dated January 12.

**Important notes**
- Use the exact city pair and date that match the segment you wish to cancel.
- If you need to cancel all waitlist segments before a certain date, use **WPNCB** (`WPNCB‡12JAN`).
- For a specific segment number, use **WPNCS** (`WPNCS‡3`).

AGENT: What does WPNCB do?
BOT:
**Answer**
`WPNCB` cancels every wait‑listed segment in a PNR that is scheduled before a specified cutoff date.

**Format & explanation**
`WPNCB‡12JAN`
- `12JAN` – the cutoff date; all wait‑list segments dated earlier than this will be removed.

**Example**
`WPNCB‡12JAN` – deletes all wait‑listed segments in the PNR that occur before January 12.

**Important notes**
- Use the exact date format (`DDMMM`).
- This command does not target a specific segment number—use `WPNCS` for that.

AGENT: How do I add a passenger to a waitlist?
BOT:
**Answer**
To add a passenger to a waitlist, use the **WPN** format.

**Format & explanation**
`WPN‡LAX/ORD‡12JAN‡HK1‡NAME/SMITH/JOHN`
- `LAX/ORD` – city pair (origin/destination)
- `12JAN` – travel date
- `HK1` – number of seats to wait‑list
- `NAME/SMITH/JOHN` – passenger name (last/first)

**Example**
`WPN‡LAX/ORD‡12JAN‡HK1‡NAME/SMITH/JOHN` – adds John Smith to the waitlist for one seat on the Los Angeles‑to‑Chicago flight on January 12.

**Important notes**
- Use the exact city pair and date that match the sold‑out flight.
- The passenger name must be in the `NAME/Last/First` format.
- `HK1` indicates how many seats the passenger is waiting for.

*Section 12.1 WPN – Waitlist PNR* in sample_sabre_guide.txt.
