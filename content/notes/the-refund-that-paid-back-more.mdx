excerpt: 'Two currency bugs in a live payments platform, and the rule that fixed both: store money in the currency it was paid in.'
date: 2026-09-30

---

Someone paid NGN 10,000 for an event on BemaHub, then cancelled. The refund landed as NGN 10,005. Another time it was NGN 10,002. Referral rewards that should have been round numbers showed up as NGN 3,007 or NGN 3,759.

Nobody lost money on purpose. The money was taking a trip.

## The first bug: a round trip through dollars

Our wallet stored everything in dollars. So a naira refund was converted to dollars at that moment's exchange rate. Later, the wallet page converted it back to naira at *that* moment's rate. Two different rates, one small drift, every time.

The fix was to stop sending money on a round trip: keep the real amount and the real currency with the transaction, and show exactly that.

## The second bug: a check that was always true

The rewards had a deeper problem. A check that was meant to ask *"were this campaign's prices set in dollars?"* was actually asking *"does this row contain any dollar figure?"*

Every campaign stores a dollar figure for bookkeeping. So all 53 naira campaigns were treated as dollar campaigns. A NGN 15,000 minimum was read as US$10.99, rewards were worked out in dollars from that, and they only came back to roughly the right naira through conversion.

After the fix, the next reward came out exactly: 18% of NGN 15,000 = NGN 2,700.

One honest caveat: older rows were not corrected, because their original amounts had never been saved. You cannot recover a number you never stored.

## Two lessons

- **Never convert money there and back.** Store what was paid, in the currency it was paid in.
- **When a check looks right, read what it actually asks.** This one was true for everything, so it could never tell anything apart.
