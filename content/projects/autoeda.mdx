## Overview

AutoEDA automatically cleans CSV datasets and generates exploratory analysis: type detection,
missing-value handling, deduplication, and visualizations, all from an upload.

## What I built

- **Automatic column-type detection** (text, categorical, numeric) with a preview where the
  user can override.
- **Cleaning**: missing-value handling and duplicate removal.
- **Visualizations**: histograms, a correlation heatmap, outlier boxplots, and bar charts.

## Proof

I fed it a 423-row sales CSV it had never seen, deliberately messy: missing ratings, two
duplicate rows, and one extreme outlier. It correctly typed all eight columns, removed both
duplicates (423 down to 421 rows), and generated the full chart set. The heatmap and other
charts above are its real output.
