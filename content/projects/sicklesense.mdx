## Overview

SickleSense is a digital health platform that predicts sickle cell pain crises and gives
patients and caregivers early, actionable guidance.

## What I built

- The **backend**: user management, health logging, early-warning logic, and JWT auth.
- **Machine-learning risk prediction** feeding crisis-risk estimation and recommendations.
- Data processing pipelines supporting the predictive models.
