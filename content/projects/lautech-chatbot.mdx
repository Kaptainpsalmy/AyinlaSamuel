## Overview

An intelligent university FAQ chatbot for LAUTECH, using TF-IDF vectorization with cosine
similarity for semantic matching.

## What I built

- **Semantic matching** with TF-IDF and cosine similarity, plus fuzzy matching for typos.
- **Confidence scoring**, fallback handlers, and adaptive learning.
- A **JWT-protected admin dashboard** with analytics and bulk import/export.
