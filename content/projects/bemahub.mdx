## Overview

BemaHub is a gospel-creator platform: it runs campaigns, a wallet and Recognized Impact
ledger, referrals, events, and payments in support of Christian music and creators. It is
**live in production** at app.bemahub.com. I own the **backend**.

Note: I engineered the backend of this platform. The campaigns and creators shown are
BEMA's, not my own content.

## What I built

- The **BMH REST API** (a WordPress plugin) as the single source of truth for business
  rules: campaign metrics, access-level resolution, referral (LoopCode) generation, and
  the Recognized Impact calculation.
- A companion **payments plugin** wiring Paystack and PayPal, with webhooks and access-grant
  triggers.
- Protected media via S3 signed URLs, and integrations for merchandise and real-time features.
- A **149-test launch-readiness verification suite** that re-derives platform state from the
  live database.

## Architecture

- **One source of truth.** Business rules live in the BMH REST API plugin; the Next.js
  frontend only displays what the API decides.
- **Payments.** A separate payments plugin handles Paystack and PayPal, their webhooks,
  and the access grants that follow a successful payment.
- **Money keeps its currency.** Each transaction stores the amount and currency that were
  actually paid, instead of converting through a base currency.
- **Prices are fixed when saved.** Exchange rates for all nine supported currencies are
  locked into a snapshot when a price is saved, so a quote never moves on its own.

## Engineering challenges

- **A NGN 10,000 refund that paid back NGN 10,005**: money converted there and back at two
  different rates, and a currency check that was true for every campaign.
  [Read the note](/notes/the-refund-that-paid-back-more).
- **Prices that changed twice a day** with nobody editing them, from level prices worked
  out at read time against a live rate. [Read the note](/notes/prices-that-changed-by-themselves).
- **Referral rewards that silently stopped for six days**, because the autoloader didn't know
  a folder existed. [Read the note](/notes/one-missing-line-stopped-referral-rewards).

## Tradeoffs

- **Stored currency over a single base currency.** Every amount carries its own currency, so
  reports have to handle several currencies, but no amount ever drifts through conversion.
- **Save-time prices over live prices.** A saved price keeps its locked rate until someone
  saves it again, in exchange for quotes that never change unexpectedly.

## Stack

PHP on WordPress, MySQL, a REST API under `/wp-json/bmh/v1`, JWT and social auth, Paystack
and PayPal, AWS S3, and WordPress Action Scheduler for background jobs.

## Lessons

- Never convert money there and back. Store what was paid, in the currency it was paid in.
- Calculate prices when they are saved, not when they are read.
- A health check that counts what is pending cannot see what is failing.
