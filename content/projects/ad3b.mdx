## Overview

AD3B is a comparison lab for four symmetric block ciphers: AES, DES, 3DES, and Blowfish.
It measures them empirically across performance and security, and the results are the data
source for the results chapter of the accompanying thesis.

## What I built

- Independent implementations of each cipher, **verified against NIST and Schneier reference
  test vectors** rather than just round-trip self-consistency.
- A **Cipher Lab**, a **Benchmark Suite**, a **Security Analysis** tab (avalanche effect, key
  space), and a **Comparison Report**.
- Controlled, repeated measurement so the comparison is fair and reproducible.

## Proof

All 16 build phases are complete, and the official results are frozen: **1600 of 1600 trials
correct**. The benchmark charts (security comparison, throughput, avalanche effect) and the
architecture diagram are shown above.
