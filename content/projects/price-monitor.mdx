## Overview

A microservice that monitors booked flights and triggers a price-drop alert when a fare falls
by 10 percent or more, built as a coding-challenge implementation for Sabre.

## What I built

- **Scheduled price checks** (APScheduler) with a mock polling mechanism.
- **Idempotent alerts**: a qualifying drop fires exactly one alert, never duplicates.
- A full **REST API** (19 endpoints) for flight management, price history, and stats.
- 80 percent or higher test coverage.

## Proof

I created a flight (LOS to LHR at £890), forced an 18 percent drop, and the alert fired at
10 percent (£801). Then I called force-drop again with the same target: it returned
`alert_triggered: false` with the alert count still at 1. The idempotency claim in the README
is real, and the run transcript is in the evidence.
