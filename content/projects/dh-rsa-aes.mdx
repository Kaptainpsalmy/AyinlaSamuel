## Overview

A second hybrid cryptosystem, this one combining Diffie-Hellman key exchange, RSA, and AES
for secure data transmission, with a sender and receiver view and a performance dashboard.

## What I built

- A **Communication** tab with a sender and receiver, showing the proposed DH-RSA-AES scheme.
- A **Performance Dashboard** and a **Security Analysis** tab.
- Attack simulation and benchmarking, with a built standalone executable.
