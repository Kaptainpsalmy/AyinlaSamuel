## Overview

SabreCWA is an assessment and test-management platform: organizations author tests,
deliver them under controlled conditions, and analyze the results. I owned the
**backend**, built with FastAPI and PostgreSQL, as part of a four-person team.

## What I built

- **Auth and roles.** JWT authentication with bcrypt, and three roles (author, admin,
  super_admin) with an admin impersonation flow for support.
- **Seven question types**, most auto-graded: single choice, multiple choice, yes/no,
  short answer, essay, file upload, and drawing.
- **Test lifecycle**: draft, active, scheduled, and closed, with open-link, group-password,
  and one-time-code access modes.
- **Server-authoritative timers** so the clock cannot be gamed from the client.
- **Anti-cheat**: tab-switch detection, copy/paste blocking, full-screen enforcement, and a
  warning system.
- **Certificates** issued automatically on passing, plus analytics (score distributions,
  per-question difficulty) and CSV export.

## Stack

FastAPI, async SQLAlchemy 2.0, Alembic migrations, PostgreSQL, JWT with bcrypt, and a
Next.js + Tailwind admin frontend.

## Proof

I ran the whole stack locally against a disposable Postgres database, seeded a demo
super-admin and a published sample test, and logged in to capture the real authenticated
admin: dashboard, test list, analytics, live monitor (with the websocket auto-refresh
indicator), results, certificates, and settings. The screenshots above are from that run.
