## Overview

A hybrid cryptosystem desktop application: it combines elliptic-curve key agreement, AES
payload encryption, and RSA signatures, and makes the entire secure exchange visible.

## What I built

- A **Secure Exchange** flow: generate keys, encrypt and send, receive and verify, with a
  **packet inspector** that shows the sender's ECC public key, the AES ciphertext, and the
  RSA signature exactly as transmitted. Private keys are never displayed.
- A **Benchmark** tab comparing performance.
- An **Attack Simulator** and a **Key Inspector**.

## Proof

I drove the real flow: the app generated an ECC keypair and RSA-2048, encrypted a message,
transmitted it, and on the receiving side verified the signature and recovered the plaintext.
The packet inspector showed the actual bytes on the wire, and the status read "verified,
plaintext recovered successfully." A complete hybrid round-trip, captured mid-flow.
